## Green Slime
**Nuisance Hazard (Levels 1–4)**

Acidic green slime devours flesh, organic material, and metal on contact. Bright green, wet, and sticky, it clings to walls, floors, and ceilings in patches.

### Mechanics
- **Coverage**: 5-foot square patches
- **Senses**: Blindsight 30 feet
- **Movement**: Drops from above when detecting movement; otherwise immobile
- **Avoidance**: DC 10 Dexterity save if aware of presence
- **Initial Damage**: 5 (1d10) Acid damage on contact
- **Ongoing Effect**: Damage repeats at start of each turn until scraped off (action required)
- **Material Damage**: 11 (2d10) Acid damage per round to wood/metal; destroys nonmagical scrapers
- **Vulnerability**: Destroyed by direct sunlight, Cold, Fire, or Radiant damage

## Inferno
**Deadly Hazard (Levels 5–10) or Nuisance Hazard (Levels 11–16)**

When an uncontrolled fire spreads, it can create an inferno that quickly engulfs creatures, objects, and vegetation.

### Mechanics
- **Minimum Size**: Four contiguous 10-foot Cubes
- **Suppression**: Each 10-foot Cube requires 10 gallons of water
- **Growth**: Strong wind for 1 minute adds 1d4 new Cubes
- **Duration**: Burns out after 1d10 minutes without fuel
- **Object Damage**: 22 (4d10) Fire damage immediately and at end of each minute (non-worn/carried items)
- **Creature Damage**: 22 (4d10) Fire damage on entering or starting turn within; inflicts burning condition

## Poisonous Gas
**Nuisance Hazard (Levels 1–4)**

Poisonous gas is usually encountered in enclosed spaces like sewers or sealed tombs. The transparent gas carries a foul odor.

### Mechanics
- **Coverage**: Up to ten 10-foot Cubes
- **Replenishment**: Continuous or periodic from natural/magical source
- **Dispersal**: Strong wind clears for 1 minute
- **Save**: DC 12 Constitution
- **Damage**: 5 (1d10) Poison damage (failed save), half on success
- **Additional Effect**: Disadvantage on Death Saving Throws while in gas

### Scaling by Level

| Levels | Poison Damage | Save DC |
|--------|--------------|---------|
| 5–10   | 11 (2d10)    | 14      |
| 11–16  | 22 (4d10)    | 16      |
| 17–20  | 55 (10d10)   | 18      |

## Quicksand Pit
**Nuisance Hazard (Levels 1–4)**

### Mechanics
- **Dimensions**: 10 feet deep, 10-foot square area
- **Initial Sink**: 1d4 + 1 feet on entering
- **Condition**: Restrained
- **Ongoing**: Sinks 1d4 feet per turn
- **Escape**: Action, Strength (Athletics) DC 10 + feet sunk
- **Full Submersion**: Total Cover, Blinded, suffocation risk
- **Rescue**: Action, Strength (Athletics) DC 5 + feet sunk

### Scaling by Level

| Levels | Pit Depth | Sinking Rate |
|--------|-----------|--------------|
| 5–10   | 15 feet   | 1d6 feet     |
| 11–16  | 20 feet   | 1d8 feet     |
| 17–20  | 30 feet   | 1d10 feet    |

## Razorvine
**Nuisance Hazard (Levels 1–4)**

Razorvine grows in wild tangles and hedges, clinging to buildings and surfaces like ivy.

### Mechanics
- **Wall/Hedge Stats**: 10-foot high, 10-foot wide, 5-foot thick
- **AC**: 11
- **HP**: 25
- **Immunities**: Bludgeoning, Piercing, Psychic damage
- **Contact Effect**: DC 10 Dexterity save or 5 (1d10) Slashing damage

## River Styx
**Nuisance Hazard (Levels 11–16)**

The River Styx courses through the Lower Planes, shattering intellect and stripping memories.

### Mechanics
- **Trigger**: Drinking, entering, or starting turn in river
- **Save**: DC 20 Intelligence
- **Damage**: 19 (3d12) Psychic damage on failed save
- **Duration**: 30 days (no spells or Magic action)
- **Immunity**: Affected creatures can swim/drink without additional effects
- **Removal**: Greater Restoration, Heal, or Wish only
- **Permanent Effect**: After 30 days, total memory loss (Wish or divine intervention required)
- **Water Potency**: Loses effect after 24 hours unless preserved by special rituals

## Rockslide
**Deadly Hazard (Levels 1–4)**

### Mechanics
- **Save**: DC 15 Dexterity
- **Failed Save**: 11 (2d10) Bludgeoning, Prone, moves with slide
- **Success**: Half damage only
- **Aftermath**: Space becomes Difficult Terrain
- **Burial**: Prone creatures gain Restrained and Total Cover
- **Escape**: Action, DC 15 Strength (Athletics) check
- **Failed Escape**: Remains buried, gains 1 Exhaustion level
- **Rescue**: 1 minute to free another creature

### Scaling by Level
- **Levels 5–10**: 22 (4d10) damage
- **Levels 11–16**: 55 (10d10) damage
- **Levels 17–20**: 99 (18d10) damage

## Collapsing Roof
**Deadly Hazard (Levels 1-4)**

### Mechanics

## Vicious Vine
**Nuisance Hazard (Levels 1–4)**

Magically animated vines that appear as ordinary plants until they move.

### Mechanics
- **Stats**: AC 11, HP 16
- **Immunities**: Bludgeoning, Piercing, Psychic damage
- **Trigger**: Within 5 feet on turn or starting turn there
- **Save**: DC 12 Dexterity or Grappled (escape DC 12)
- **Damage**: 5 (1d10) Necrotic per turn while grappled
- **Capacity**: One creature at a time
- **Special**: DC 10 Charisma (Persuasion) with Speak with Plants for release

### Scaling by Level

| Levels | Necrotic Damage | Save/Escape DC |
|--------|----------------|----------------|
| 5–10   | 11 (2d10)      | 14             |
| 11–16  | 22 (4d10)      | 16             |
| 17–20  | 55 (10d10)     | 18             |

## Webs
**Nuisance Hazard (Levels 1–4)**

## Yellow Mold
**Deadly Hazard (Levels 1–4) or Nuisance Hazard (Levels 5–10)**

Yellow mold grows in dark places and releases toxic spores when disturbed.

### Mechanics
- **Coverage**: 5-foot square patches
- **Trigger**: Touch releases spore cloud (10-foot Cube)
- **Save**: DC 15 Constitution
- **Initial Damage**: 11 (2d10) Poison damage, Poisoned for 1 minute
- **Ongoing**: 5 (1d10) Poison damage per turn while Poisoned
- **Recovery**: Save at end of each turn to end effect
- **Vulnerability**: Destroyed by direct sunlight or any Fire damage




Deep Water
Swimming through deep water (more than 100 feet deep) presents additional challenges because of the water’s pressure and cold temperature. After each hour of swimming in deep water, a creature that lacks a Swim Speed must succeed on a DC 10 Constitution saving throw or gain 1 Exhaustion level.

Extreme Cold
When the temperature is 0 degrees Fahrenheit or lower, a creature exposed to the extreme cold must succeed on a DC 10 Constitution saving throw at the end of each hour or gain 1 Exhaustion level. Creatures that have Resistance or Immunity to Cold damage automatically succeed on the save.

Extreme Heat
When the temperature is 100 degrees Fahrenheit or higher, a creature exposed to the extreme heat and without access to drinkable water must succeed on a Constitution saving throw at the end of each hour or gain 1 Exhaustion level. The DC is 5 for the first hour and increases by 1 for each additional hour. Creatures wearing Medium or Heavy armor have Disadvantage on the save. Creatures that have Resistance or Immunity to Fire damage automatically succeed on the save.

Frigid Water
A creature can be immersed in frigid water for a number of minutes equal to its Constitution score before suffering any ill effects. Each additional minute spent in frigid water requires the creature to succeed on a DC 10 Constitution saving throw or gain 1 Exhaustion level. Creatures with Resistance or Immunity to Cold damage automatically succeed on the save, as do creatures that are naturally adapted to living in ice-cold water.
