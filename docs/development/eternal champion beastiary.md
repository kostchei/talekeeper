# Eternal Champion Bestiary - D&D 5e SRD Matches

Based on creatures and monsters from Michael Moorcock's Eternal Champion series, matched to D&D 5e SRD creatures by characteristics and description.

---

## DRAGONS & FLYING CREATURES

### Phoorn (Melnibonéan Dragons)
**Source:** Elric series
**Description:** Serpentine bodies with 30-foot wingspans, forked tongues, cold eyes, teeth like swords, multicolored iridescent scales. Spit highly flammable venomous saliva instead of breathing fire.
**D&D 5e Match:** **Wyvern** (venomous flying reptile) or **Young Dragon** (various colors based on scale description)

### Wild Flying Beasts of Bronze
**Source:** Corum series
**Description:** Bronze-colored flying creatures
**D&D 5e Match:** **Gargoyle** or **Animated Armor** (flying construct variant)

---

## GIANTS & LARGE HUMANOIDS

### Grahluk
**Source:** Elric series (The Vanishing Tower)
**Description:** Large, furry, ape-like humanoids standing 10 feet tall and very broad. Carry nets, ropes, and shields. Devolved creatures with vestigial intelligence, driven to hunt their ancient enemies the Elenoin.
**D&D 5e Match:** **Ogre** or **Ettin**

### Fhoi Myore (The Cold Folk)
**Source:** Corum series (Silver Hand trilogy)
**Description:** Seven powerful but diseased and barely sentient giants from Limbo. Twisted, dying gods bringing eternal winter. Find existence itself painful.
**D&D 5e Match:** **Frost Giant** or **Cloud Giant** (diseased/corrupted variant)

### Blue Giants of the Argzoon
**Source:** Kane of Old Mars trilogy
**Description:** Terrible blue giants whose ravaging hordes threaten entire planets.
**D&D 5e Match:** **Hill Giant** or **Stone Giant**

### Crimson Cattle
**Source:** Corum series
**Description:** Herds of roaring crimson cattle taller than castles
**D&D 5e Match:** **Mammoth** or **Behemoth** (homebrew variant of Tarrasque if truly castle-sized)

## HUMANOID WARRIORS & SOLDIERS

### Melnibonéan Warriors
**Source:** Elric series
**Description:** Human soldiers of the ancient empire of Melniboné, dragon riders and sorcerers
**D&D 5e Match:** **Knight** or **Veteran**

### Pan Tang Sorcerers
**Source:** Elric series
**Description:** Human sorcerers and priests of Chaos from the island nation of Pan Tang
**D&D 5e Match:** **Mage** or **Cult Fanatic**

### Granbretan Beast-Masked Warriors
**Source:** Hawkmoon series
**Description:** Human aristocracy and soldiers wearing totemic animal masks at all times (Wolf, Boar, Crow, Mantis, Fly, Ape, Goat, Rat, Hound orders)
**D&D 5e Match:** **Guard**, **Knight**, or **Veteran** (various orders, same basic stats)

### Elven Infantry / Human Knights / etc.
**Source:** Various
**Description:** Standard fantasy military forces
**D&D 5e Match:** **Guard**, **Knight**, **Scout**, **Veteran** (as appropriate)

## MONSTROUS HUMANOIDS & BESTIAL CREATURES

### Elenoin
**Source:** Elric series (The Vanishing Tower)
**Description:** Appear as tall, beautiful nude women with long red hair, mouths full of sharp fangs, wield swords. Seemingly mindless or insane, devolved creatures who hunt the Grahluk.
**D&D 5e Match:** **Vampire Spawn** or **Banshee**

### Olab
**Source:** Elric series
**Description:** Reptilian creatures wielding large clubs embedded with crystalline rock discs
**D&D 5e Match:** **Lizardfolk** or **Troglodyte**

### Yaksha Tower Creatures
**Source:** Kane of Old Mars trilogy
**Description:** Ghoulish, degenerate creatures infesting ancient towers, leaving mounds of corpses
**D&D 5e Match:** **Ghoul** or **Ghast**

### D-Beasts (Dire Beasts)
**Source:** Hawkmoon series
**Description:** Animals modified by Granbretan science
**D&D 5e Match:** Various **Dire** variants (Dire Wolf, etc.)

## DEMONS & FIENDS

### Quaolnargn
**Source:** Elric series
**Description:** Demon appearing as an obscene toad-thing with slimy claws, huge gaping mouth, towers over humans. Feeds on minds and souls of adults.
**D&D 5e Match:** **Hezrou** (toad-like demon)

### Stormbringer & Sword-Demons
**Source:** Elric series
**Description:** Thousands of identical demons taking the form of black runeswords, agents of Chaos that feed on souls
**D&D 5e Match:** **Sentient magic weapon possessed by demon** - no direct match, but similar to cursed items

### Pyaray
**Source:** Elric series
**Description:** Octopus demon commanding Chaos Ships
**D&D 5e Match:** **Kraken** (demonic variant)

### Various Lesser Demons
**Source:** All series
**Description:** Shapeshifting demons summoned by sorcerers for various tasks
**D&D 5e Match:** **Quasit**, **Imp**, **Dretch**, **Barlgura**, **Vrock** (various based on power level)

### Lords of Chaos (Dukes of Hell)
**Source:** Elric series
**Description:** God-like beings with demon appearance and behavior. Prone to shapeshifting, violent mood swings. Include Arioch (Lord of Seven Darks), Xiombarg (Queen of Swords, rides lion-bull creature)
**D&D 5e Match:** **Balor**, **Pit Fiend**, or **Demon Lord** (Demogorgon, Orcus, etc.)

## ELEMENTALS & ELEMENTAL LORDS

### Standard Elementals
**Source:** All series
**Description:** Spirits of Air, Earth, Fire, and Water summoned by sorcerers
**D&D 5e Match:** **Air Elemental**, **Earth Elemental**, **Fire Elemental**, **Water Elemental**

### Elemental Kings
**Source:** Elric series
**Description:** Gods of the elements: Straasha (Water), Grome (Earth), Kakatal (Fire), Misha (Air), Darnizhaan (Earth)
**D&D 5e Match:** **Elder Elemental** (homebrew) or **Elemental Myrmidon** (Mordenkainen's)

## BEAST LORDS (ANIMAL DEITIES)

**Source:** Elric series
**Description:** Patron deities of animal types: Fileet (Lady of Birds), Haaashastaak (Lord of Lizards), Meerclar (Lady of Cats), Nnuuurrr'c'c (Lord of Insect Swarm), Nuru-ah (Lord of Cattle), Roofdrak (Lord of Dogs)
**D&D 5e Match:** Use stats for various beasts' most powerful forms:
- **Fileet:** Giant Eagle or Roc
- **Haaashastaak:** Giant Lizard or Dragon (green/black)
- **Meerclar:** Displacer Beast or Saber-Toothed Tiger
- **Nnuuurrr'c'c:** Insect Plague effect or Ankheg
- **Nuru-ah:** Mammoth or Bulette
- **Roofdrak:** Dire Wolf or Hell Hound
