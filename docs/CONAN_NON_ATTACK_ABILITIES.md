# Non-Attack Monster Abilities - Conan Campaign Frame

## Overview
This document catalogs monster abilities that do NOT involve "to hit" attack rolls. These abilities include breath weapons, charm/domination effects, buff/debuff abilities, recharge powers, and other strategic combat actions that change the battlefield situation without standard attack mechanics.

## Ability Categories

### 1. BREATH WEAPONS (Recharge Mechanics)
Breath weapons cannot be used every round and require recharge rolls.

#### Dragons
- **Acid Breath** (Black, Copper Dragons)
  - Recharge 5-6 (roll d6 at start of turn)
  - Area effect: 60-90 ft. line or cone
  - Dexterity save for half damage
  - Example: Adult Black Dragon deals 54 (12d8) Acid damage

- **Lightning Breath** (Blue, Bronze Dragons)
  - Recharge 5-6
  - 90-120 ft. line
  - Dexterity save DC 19-23
  - Example: Adult Blue Dragon deals 66 (12d10) Lightning damage

- **Fire Breath** (Red, Gold, Brass Dragons)
  - Recharge 5-6
  - 90 ft. cone
  - Dexterity save
  - Massive fire damage

- **Poison Breath** (Green Dragons)
  - Recharge 5-6
  - 60 ft. cone
  - Constitution save
  - Poison damage + potential ongoing effects

- **Special Breath Weapons**:
  - **Sleep Breath** (Brass Dragon) - Constitution save or fall unconscious
  - **Repulsion Breath** (Bronze Dragon) - Strength save or pushed back
  - **Slowing Breath** (Copper Dragon) - Constitution save or speed halved
  - **Weakening Breath** (Gold Dragon) - Strength save or disadvantage on Strength checks

#### Other Creatures
- **Ankheg: Acid Spray** (Recharge 6)
  - 30 ft. line
  - Dexterity save DC 13
  - 10 (3d6) Acid damage

- **Behir: Lightning Breath** (Recharge 5-6)
  - 20 ft. line
  - Dexterity save DC 16
  - 66 (12d10) Lightning damage

### 2. CHARM & DOMINATION ABILITIES
Mind-control and manipulation effects that turn enemies into allies.

#### Aboleth
- **Dominate Mind** (2/Day - Limited Use)
  - Wisdom save DC 16
  - Range: 30 ft.
  - Effect: Target becomes charmed ally under aboleth's control
  - Duration: Until aboleth dies or is on different plane
  - Target can save again when taking damage or after 24 hours away

- **Consume Memories**
  - Intelligence save DC 16
  - Only works on Charmed or Grappled creatures
  - 10 (3d6) Psychic damage
  - Gains target's memories if reduced to 0 HP

#### Vampire
- **Charm**
  - Wisdom save DC 17
  - Range: 30 ft.
  - Target regards vampire as trusted friend
  - Lasts 24 hours or until vampire harms target

### 3. FEAR & FRIGHTENED CONDITIONS
Abilities that cause enemies to flee or become paralyzed with terror.

#### Dragons (Frightful Presence)
- **Adult/Ancient Dragons**
  - Wisdom save DC 16-24
  - Range: 120 ft.
  - Effect: Frightened for 1 minute
  - Target can repeat save at end of each turn
  - Immune for 24 hours if successful

#### Doppelganger
- **Unsettling Visage** (Recharge 6)
  - Wisdom save DC 11
  - Range: 30 ft.
  - Effect: Frightened until end of doppelganger's next turn

### 4. PARALYSIS & IMMOBILIZATION
Effects that prevent targets from taking actions.

#### Ghoul
- **Paralysis** (built into Claw attack)
  - Constitution save DC 10
  - Effect: Paralyzed for 1 minute
  - Target can repeat save at end of each turn
  - Elves have advantage

#### Chuul
- **Paralytic Tentacles** (with Pincer grapple)
  - Constitution save DC 13
  - Effect: Poisoned for 1 minute
  - While poisoned: Paralyzed
  - Target can repeat save at end of each turn

### 5. PETRIFICATION
Turning creatures to stone.

#### Basilisk
- **Petrifying Gaze**
  - Constitution save DC 12
  - Range: 30 ft. (if target can see basilisk)
  - Failure: Begins to turn to stone (Restrained)
  - Must save again at end of next turn
  - Second Failure: Petrified (turned to stone)

#### Medusa
- **Petrifying Gaze**
  - Constitution save DC 14
  - Range: 30 ft.
  - Similar mechanics to Basilisk but higher DC

### 6. INVISIBILITY & STEALTH
Abilities that make creatures unseen.

#### Darkmantle
- **Darkness Aura** (1/Day - Limited Use)
  - 15 ft. radius of magical darkness
  - Lasts 10 minutes
  - Can see normally within own darkness

#### Invisible Stalker
- **Invisibility** (inherent)
  - Permanently invisible
  - Only becomes visible when killed

### 7. TELEPORTATION & DIMENSION DOOR
Movement abilities that bypass normal travel.

#### Blink Dog
- **Teleport** (Recharge 4-6)
  - Range: 40 ft.
  - Can teleport before or after attacking
  - No opportunity attacks

### 8. SUMMONING & CONJURATION
Calling reinforcements or creating hazards.

#### Demons/Devils
- **Summon Demon/Devil** (1/Day)
  - Chance-based: Roll percentage dice
  - Example: Glabrezu can summon 1d6 vrocks (50% chance)
  - Summoned creatures can't summon more

### 9. POLYMORPH & SHAPECHANGING
Transforming self or others.

#### Succubus/Incubus
- **Shapechange**
  - Can change into Small or Medium humanoid
  - Or revert to true form
  - Statistics remain the same
  - Equipment doesn't change

#### Couatl
- **Shapechange**
  - Can polymorph into humanoid or beast (CR 4 or lower)
  - Or revert to true form

### 10. ENLARGE/REDUCE EFFECTS
Size-changing abilities.

#### Duergar
- **Enlarge** (Recharge 4-6)
  - Self-cast, no save
  - Duration: 1 minute
  - Becomes Large size
  - Advantage on Strength checks/saves
  - +1d4 extra weapon damage

### 11. SWALLOW & ENGULF
Consuming enemies whole.

#### Behir
- **Swallow**
  - Automatic if target is Medium or smaller and grappled
  - Target takes 21 (6d6) Acid damage per turn
  - Blinded and Restrained while swallowed
  - If behir takes 30+ damage in one turn, must make DC 14 Con save or regurgitate

#### Purple Worm
- **Swallow**
  - Automatic if target is Large or smaller and grappled
  - 21 (6d6) Acid damage per turn
  - Blinded and Restrained
  - If worm takes 30+ damage, must save or regurgitate

### 12. LEGENDARY ACTIONS
Actions taken outside normal turn order.

#### Ancient Dragons
- **Detect** (Legendary Action - Costs 1)
  - Make Wisdom (Perception) check

- **Tail Attack** (Legendary Action - Costs 1)
  - Make one tail attack

- **Wing Attack** (Legendary Action - Costs 2)
  - All creatures within 15 ft. make Dexterity save
  - 16 (2d6+9) Bludgeoning damage + knocked prone
  - Dragon can fly half its speed

### 13. LAIR ACTIONS
Environmental effects in creature's lair.

#### Dragons (in Lair)
- **Lair Action** (Initiative 20)
  - Choose one effect:
    - Pools of water become acid
    - Grasping roots erupt from ground
    - Volcanic gases fill area
    - Fog clouds area

## IMPLEMENTATION REQUIREMENTS

### Phase 1: Recharge Abilities
**Priority: HIGH** - Breath weapons are iconic

#### Required Systems:
1. **Recharge Tracker**
   - Track recharge status per ability
   - Roll d6 at start of creature's turn
   - Mark ability as available on 5-6 (or 4-6, 3-6 depending on ability)

2. **UI Indicators**
   - Show "RECHARGE" status on monster action cards
   - Display recharge dice result in combat log
   - Gray out unavailable abilities

3. **Save-Based Damage**
   - Area of effect targeting
   - Dexterity/Constitution/etc. saves
   - Half damage on successful save
   - Automatic damage application

#### Example Implementation:
```python
class MonsterAbility:
    def __init__(self, name, recharge_type=None):
        self.name = name
        self.recharge_type = recharge_type  # "5-6", "4-6", "6", or None
        self.is_available = True

    def attempt_recharge(self):
        if self.recharge_type is None:
            return True

        roll = random.randint(1, 6)
        if self.recharge_type == "5-6":
            self.is_available = roll >= 5
        elif self.recharge_type == "4-6":
            self.is_available = roll >= 4
        elif self.recharge_type == "6":
            self.is_available = roll == 6

        return self.is_available
```

### Phase 2: Limited Use Abilities (X/Day)
**Priority: HIGH** - Dominate Mind, Darkness Aura, etc.

#### Required Systems:
1. **Usage Tracker**
   - Track uses remaining per ability
   - Reset on long rest
   - Display "2/Day (1 remaining)" in UI

2. **Database Schema**
   ```sql
   CREATE TABLE monster_ability_uses (
       encounter_id TEXT,
       monster_id TEXT,
       ability_name TEXT,
       uses_remaining INTEGER,
       max_uses INTEGER
   );
   ```

### Phase 3: Charm/Domination
**Priority: MEDIUM** - Complex but impactful

#### Required Systems:
1. **Mind Control State**
   - Track charmed/dominated creatures
   - Change targeting rules (can't attack controller)
   - May fight for controller

2. **Save Repetition**
   - Save again when taking damage
   - Save again after time period (24 hours)
   - Track save DC and timing

3. **UI Changes**
   - Show "CHARMED" status on character sheet
   - Modify action card availability
   - Display controller relationship

### Phase 4: Fear & Frightened
**Priority: MEDIUM** - Common condition

#### Required Systems:
1. **Frightened Condition** (already partially implemented)
   - Disadvantage on ability checks and attacks
   - Can't move closer to source
   - Save at end of each turn

2. **Frightful Presence**
   - Area effect (120 ft. radius)
   - Wisdom save
   - 24-hour immunity if successful

### Phase 5: Paralysis & Petrification
**Priority: MEDIUM** - Save-or-lose mechanics

#### Required Systems:
1. **Paralyzed Condition**
   - Can't move or speak
   - Auto-fail Strength and Dexterity saves
   - Attacks have advantage
   - Melee attacks are auto-crits

2. **Petrified Condition**
   - Turned to stone
   - Incapacitated
   - Resistance to all damage
   - Weight increases 10x
   - Immune to poison/disease

3. **Progressive Saves**
   - First fail: Restrained
   - Second fail: Petrified
   - Track save progression

### Phase 6: Legendary Actions
**Priority: LOW** - Complex turn order changes

#### Required Systems:
1. **Action Economy Extension**
   - Track legendary action points (3 per round)
   - Use between other creatures' turns
   - Reset at start of creature's turn

2. **UI Turn Indicator**
   - Show when legendary actions can be used
   - Display remaining points
   - Prompt for action selection

### Phase 7: Lair Actions
**Priority: LOW** - Environmental effects

#### Required Systems:
1. **Initiative 20 Actions**
   - Trigger at fixed initiative count
   - Area effects
   - No save for most (automatic environment change)

2. **Environmental Hazards**
   - Track hazard zones
   - Apply damage/effects automatically
   - Visual indicators on battlefield

## DATABASE SCHEMA ADDITIONS

```sql
-- New table for tracking ability states
CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS monster_ability_tracker (
    encounter_id TEXT NOT NULL,
    monster_id TEXT NOT NULL,
    ability_name TEXT NOT NULL,
    ability_type TEXT NOT NULL,  -- 'recharge', 'limited_use', 'legendary'
    recharge_requirement TEXT,   -- '5-6', '4-6', '6', etc.
    max_uses INTEGER,             -- for X/Day abilities
    uses_remaining INTEGER,       -- current uses left
    is_available BOOLEAN,         -- current availability
    last_recharge_roll INTEGER,   -- for debugging
    PRIMARY KEY (encounter_id, monster_id, ability_name)
);

-- Track ongoing effects from abilities
CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS monster_ability_effects (
    effect_id TEXT PRIMARY KEY,
    encounter_id TEXT NOT NULL,
    source_monster_id TEXT NOT NULL,
    ability_name TEXT NOT NULL,
    target_id TEXT NOT NULL,        -- character or monster
    effect_type TEXT NOT NULL,      -- 'charmed', 'frightened', 'paralyzed', 'petrified'
    save_dc INTEGER,
    duration_type TEXT,             -- 'rounds', 'minutes', 'until_damage', 'permanent'
    duration_remaining INTEGER,
    can_repeat_save BOOLEAN,
    save_ability TEXT,              -- 'WIS', 'CON', 'DEX', etc.
    created_round INTEGER,
    FOREIGN KEY (encounter_id) REFERENCES encounters(encounter_id)
);
```

## NOTES

### Design Principles
1. **Keep it Simple**: Start with recharge and limited use
2. **Visual Clarity**: Players must see what abilities are available
3. **Save Transparency**: Show DC, ability, and result clearly
4. **Effect Tracking**: Clear indication of active conditions
5. **Conan Flavor**: Prioritize sorcery, poison, and brutal effects

### Performance Considerations
- Recharge rolls are quick (1d6)
- Save checks reuse existing save system
- Effect tracking needs efficient queries
- UI updates should be reactive (signals/slots)

### User Experience
- Player should understand why breath weapon didn't recharge
- Clear indication when ability is ready again
- Tooltips explaining condition effects
- Combat log shows all ability uses and results
