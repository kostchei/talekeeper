# Loot Plan

## Basic Rules
1. **Treasure only drops if you can use it** - must check class features for proficiency (may need more database info for proficiency checks)
2. **Distribution follows core rules** - % chance of rare etc but from limited list
3. **No duplicates** - if already equipped/in inventory, you don't get another. Fill "best in slot" then get random gear from remaining items (potions, etc.)

## Common Items
- 1st level spell scroll
- Potion of healing
- Silvered weapon
- **Weapons** (based on stats):
  - Rapier (dex > str, main hand, dueling)
  - Longsword (str > dex, main hand, 2handed, dueling)
  - Greatsword (str > dex, 2handed, great weapon fighting)
  - Greataxe (str > dex, 2handed, savage attacker)
  - Staff (main hand, 2handed)
  - Scimitar (dex > str, main/offhand, two weapon fighting)
  - Spear (main hand, 2handed)
- **Armor**: Studded leather, Chainmail, Scalemail (only if not already possessed)

## Uncommon Items
- 2nd + 3rd level spell scrolls
- Potion of Healing (Greater)
- Luckstone
- Shield +1 (or scimitar, greataxe, greatsword, spear, staff)
- **Weapon +1** (weapon list: rapier, longsword, greatsword, greataxe, scimitar, wand of the warmage, book of the devout, rod of the pact etc)
- **Protection gear**:
  - Cloak of Protection (if +3 dex or above, unarmored defence, or not prof with medium/heavy armor)
  - Otherwise: Adamantine Breastplate (proficient in stealth), Adamantine Halfplate (medium armor only), Adamantine Platemail (heavy armor)
- **Armor**: Breast Plate, Half Plate, Splint Armor, Plate Armor
- Bag of Holding


## Rare Items
- **Armor**: +1 platemail, +1 studded leather, Elven chainmail (needs 13 str, AC 16, no prof or dex required), elven chain vest (warlock/wizard)
- **Weapons**: +2 Weapon (weapon list), Executioner's axe (with shield: battleaxe, without: greataxe), vicious weapon
- **Protection**: +2 shield, bracers of defense (wizard/barbarian), off-hand weapon
- Ring of protection
- Potion of Healing (Superior)
- 4th + 5th level spell scrolls
- Ring of spell storing
- Ioun stones
- Belt of Hill Giant Strength
- Cloak of displacement
- Ring of resistance

## Very Rare Items
- **Armor**: +2 platemail, +2 studded leather
- **Weapons**: +3 weapon (weapon list), Illusionist's Bracers (any spellcaster), Staff of Power
- **Protection**: +3 shield or off weapon/wand etc
- Potion of Healing Supreme
- 6th + 7th level spell scrolls
- Ioun stones
- Belt of Stone Giant Strength
- Manual of *, Tome of * (+2 to highest stat, can only be used in town at level up)

## Legendary Items
- **Armor**: +3 platemail, +3 studded leather, robe of the archmagi
- **Weapons**: Holy Avenger, Vorpal Sword, Sword of Answering, Defender, Staff of the Magi
- Quest Maps for artifacts
- 8th + 9th level spell scrolls
- Belt of Cloud Giant Strength
- Deck of Many things

## Crafting Costs & Time

| Item Rarity | Time     | Cost      |
|-------------|----------|-----------|
| Common      | 5 days   | 50 GP     |
| Uncommon    | 10 days  | 200 GP    |
| Rare        | 50 days  | 2,000 GP  |
| Very Rare   | 125 days | 20,000 GP |
| Legendary   | 250 days | 100,000 GP|

## Special Notes
- **Amulet of health, gloves of ogre power** - ignore if you have 18, 19 or 20 in the stat (niche - maybe for quest chain?)
- **Dragon Slaying, Giant Slaying** - niche items for specific quest chains
