## Combat Data Extraction
Combat encounters from each story are being extracted and stored in `conan_combats.json`

### Data Structure Per Combat
- Flavor quote from the text
- Number of combatants
- **Suggested D&D 5e 2024 Monsters** - Recommended stat blocks for each combatant type
- Combatant descriptions (with roles and counts)
- Weapons/attacks used
- Armor/defenses
- Setting and situation
- Outcome of the combat

## D&D 5e 2024 Monster Mapping

All 34 combat encounters now include suggested D&D monster stat blocks:

### Common Monster Suggestions by Type

**Conan Stat Blocks:**
- Young Conan (thief): Gladiator (CR 5)
- King Conan: Champion (CR 9) or Warlord (CR 12)
- Berserker Conan: Gladiator with rage features

**Humanoid Combatants:**
- Guards/Soldiers: Guard (CR 1/8), Veteran (CR 3), Knight (CR 3)
- Desert Warriors: Tribal Warrior (CR 1/8), Berserker (CR 2)
- Pirates: Bandit (CR 1/8), Thug (CR 1/2), Bandit Captain (CR 2)
- Thieves: Spy (CR 1), Assassin (CR 8)
- Nobles: Noble (CR 1/8)

**Spellcasters:**
- Evil Priests: Cultist (CR 1/8), Cult Fanatic (CR 2), Priest (CR 2)
- Sorcerers: Mage (CR 6), Archmage (CR 12)
- Ancient Wizards: Archmage (CR 12) or Lich (CR 21)

**Monsters:**
- Giant Serpents: Giant Constrictor Snake (CR 2) scaled up
- Spiders: Giant Spider (CR 1), Phase Spider (CR 3)
- Apes: Ape (CR 1/2), intelligent Ogre (CR 2)
- Lions: Lion (CR 1)
- Dragons: Young Green Dragon (CR 8), Tyrannosaurus Rex (CR 8)

**Aberrations & Horrors:**
- Lovecraftian Entities: Star Spawn Hulk (CR 10), Shoggoth (CR 10)
- Alien Creatures: Gray Slaad (CR 9), Gibbering Mouther (CR 2)
- Dungeon Horrors: Otyugh (CR 5)

**Demons & Undead:**
- Summoned Demons: Glabrezu (CR 9), Nalfeshnee (CR 13)
- Winged Demons: Vrock (CR 6), Chasme (CR 6)
- Life-Drainers: Vampire (CR 13)
